Ila & Tyagi · Moodboard 06 · Shahi Shaadi

Velvet outside,
marigold inside.

This combines the two directions. The premium shell stays from the current board: the maroon velvet envelope, the Ganesha seal, the painted Magnific scenes and the bride, groom and phere motion clips. Inside, the festive Hindu layer comes back from the first version. That means swinging marigold torans, the kalash, flickering diyas, mandala knots, every rasm from haldi to reception, the baraat, the seven vows, the kids' note and the Meerut guide. Hindi still appears only in ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥.

01 · The blend

What each version brings

From the royal board

  • Velvet envelope with a gold Ganesha wax seal and glowing paisley lines
  • An ivory card that rises with the gold Ganesha and the vandana
  • Painted Magnific art: couple, phere, baraat, mandap, venue sketch
  • Motion clips, torn-paper chapters, shehnai toggle, RSVP
  • Corinthia script names and Kalnia dates

From the first Hindu invite

  • Marigold and mango-leaf torans that swing at the top of screens
  • Mangal kalash, flickering diyas, slowly spinning mandala knots
  • Marigold countdown medallions and falling petals
  • Full rasmein with animated icons: haldi, mehndi, baraat, phere, reception
  • Seven vows lit one by one, the kids' note, the Meerut travel guide

Rule: velvet maroon and gold set the luxury. Marigold, haldi and mehndi green only appear as festive accents, so the invite feels royal and joyful without looking busy.
