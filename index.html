The city of light

Welcome to Kashi

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Open in Maps
Fly
Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS) at Babatpur, about 25 km from the city
Train
Varanasi Junction or Banaras station. The Vande Bharat from New Delhi takes about 8 hours.
Weather
December nights drop to around 8°C, and morning fog can delay trains and flights. Pack a shawl and keep some buffer.
See
The Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat every evening, and a sunrise boat ride along the ghats
Darshan
Kashi Vishwanath Temple
Taste
Malaiyyo, the winter-only morning sweet, plus kachori-sabzi and a Banarasi paan
